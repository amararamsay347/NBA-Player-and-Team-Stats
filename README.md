# NBA Player and Team Statistics

## Dataset and Source 
The dataset consists of two CSV files containing historical NBA basketball statistics, with recorded dates ranging from November 1946 to October 2026:
- PlayerStatistics.csv: 1,673,921 rows and 40 columns, containing player game records with variables such as points, assists, rebounds, minutes played, shooting percentages, and turnovers.
- TeamStatistics.csv: 146,768 rows and 59 columns, containing team game records with variables such as scores, shooting percentages, rebounds, turnovers, home or away status, and game outcomes.

Source: [NBA Dataset: Box Scores and Stats (1947 - Today)](https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/eoinamoore/historical-nba-data-and-player-box-scores)

## Purpose and Relevance

The purpose of this dataset is to analyze player and team performance and identify statistical patterns associated with winning basketball games. The two files support analysis at both the individual and team levels. This makes the dataset relevant to sports analytics, where statistical evidence can help evaluate players, compare teams, and understand changes in playing styles over time.

## Research Questions
1. How do shooting percentage, rebounds, and turnovers differ between winning and losing teams?
2. How does playing at home versus away relate to a team’s winning percentage?
3. What relationship exists between a player’s minutes played and their points, assists, and rebounds?

## Selection Requirements
The dataset exceeds the minimum size requirements, with over 1.6 million player records and 146,000 team records, containing 40 and 59 columns respectively. It includes numerical variables such as points and rebounds, categorical variables such as team names, and sufficient information for EDA and questions about factors associated with winning.It comes from an identifiable publisher on Kaggle, has a reported CC0 public-domain license, and contains public sports statistics without sensitive personal information.
